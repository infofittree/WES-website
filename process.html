Our Process

Project Delivery Approach

A structured six-step process from assessment to ongoing support.

1
Step 1

Assess & Feasibility

Review inlet gas pressures, daily demand (SCM/day), peak hourly requirements, vehicle fleet mix, plot dimensions, and utility availability.

Output: FEED & Site Assessment Report
2
Step 2

Engineer & Design

Develop the design basis, plot plan, hazardous area classification, P&ID, electrical load calculations, and tailored equipment sizing.

Output: Approved Engineering Drawing Package
3
Step 3

Procure & Supply

Source OEM compressor packages, ASME storage cascades, dispensers, and certified valves, managing shipping, port logistics, and customs clearance.

Output: FAT Certificates & On-Site Delivery
4
Step 4

Construct & Install

Supervise reinforced civil foundations, blast protection barriers, stainless steel high-pressure tube fabrication, and electrical explosion-proof cabling.

Output: Mechanical & Electrical Completion
5
Step 5

Commission & Handover

Conduct hydrostatic testing, pneumatic nitrogen purging, instrument calibration, live gas-in safety testing, and formal operator training.

Output: DPR Sign-off & Handover Certificate
6
Step 6

Operate & Support

Execute preventive maintenance schedules, spare parts stocking in Nigeria, 24/7 on-call engineering dispatch, and automated uptime tracking.

Output: Long-term SLA & Uptime Guarantee

Tailored Assessment

Each project assessed against gas supply conditions, throughput, vehicle mix, filling profile and site dimensions.

Project-Specific Engineering

Final equipment sizing and system configuration established through project-specific engineering.

Handover & Support

Testing, start-up, documentation, operator training and ongoing maintenance planning.
