Company Overview

Westmount Energy Solutions provides engineering and project support for Compressed Natural Gas (CNG) infrastructure. Our project scope can be tailored to transport, retail, fleet and industrial applications, subject to project requirements, engineering review and applicable local approvals.

Our Focus

We support CNG infrastructure projects across Nigeria, covering project planning, equipment supply, installation, commissioning and lifecycle service support. From mother stations to retail dispensing, we deliver project-specific recommendations rather than one-size-fits-all configurations.

Our Commitment

A coordinated partner for a broad range of CNG infrastructure needs, focused on safe operation, reliability, maintainability and practical site execution. We support you across equipment integration, commissioning and after-sales requirements.
