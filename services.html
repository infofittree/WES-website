Services

Our Services

Complete CNG infrastructure solutions tailored to your project requirements.

CNG Mother Station

1. CNG Mother Stations

Turnkey compression, gas treatment, metering, high-pressure storage and distribution interfaces for primary pipeline connections across Nigeria.

  • Gas compression and pre-treatment packages up to 250 bar
  • High-pressure storage cascades and high-speed tube trailer filling
  • Fiscal custody transfer metering and automated PLC safety shutdown
Compression Virtual Pipeline Metering
CNG Daughter Station

2. CNG Daughter Stations

Receiving and dispensing infrastructure for sites supplied by approved mobile cascade trailers.

  • CNG decanting posts & cascade connection headers
  • Fast-fill and time-fill dispensing dispensers
  • Automated sequencing and pressure reduction systems
CNG Booster Station

3. CNG Booster Stations

Booster compressor packages and pressure management equipment where required by pipeline or cascade pressure drop.

  • Hydraulic and reciprocating booster compressor packages
  • Automated pressure regulation & interstage cooling
  • Hazardous area electrical explosion-proof panels
Retail & Fleet Dispensing

4. CNG Retail & Fleet Dispensing

Fast-fill and time-fill configurations engineered for heavy duty trucks, passenger buses, and commercial fleets.

  • High-flow NGV1 and NGV2 dispensing nozzles
  • Electronic mass flow metering with temperature compensation
  • Integrated point-of-sale and fleet RFID management
EPC Solutions

5. Turnkey EPC Solutions

Comprehensive engineering, procurement, and construction coordination including civil, mechanical, and electrical interfaces.

  • FEED, HAZOP review, and detailed engineering design
  • OEM equipment procurement and logistics clearance in Nigeria
  • Civil blast walls, mechanical piping, and electrical grounding
Commissioning & Handover

6. Commissioning & Handover

Pre-commissioning checks, pressure testing, start-up support, safety documentation, and operator training.

  • Pneumatic & hydrostatic pressure integrity verification
  • Dry run testing, nitrogen purging, and gas-in procedures
  • Comprehensive handover manuals and certified operator training
Operation & Maintenance

7. Operation & Maintenance

Preventive maintenance planning, rapid on-site troubleshooting, spare parts inventory, and SLA technical service.

  • Scheduled valve, filter, and seal overhaul programs
  • In-country spare parts warehouse in Nigeria
  • Emergency technician dispatch and remote telemetry monitoring
